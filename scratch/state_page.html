About Us
Our Second Chance Tax Relief program could provide a fresh start for single parents, divorcees, young families, and others facing unforseen financial challenges.
Life often brings us challenges we don’t expect and didn’t plan for. A divorce. A new baby. Being left as a single parent due to separation, death, or even incarceration. Any of these events may be out of your control and, as a result, may send your finances spiraling into an abyss. It’s hard to face unpaid tax debt when you are also battling other challenges, living paycheck to paycheck, and wondering how you — and your family —will make it another day. We understand. And we are ready to connect you with tax experts who can help you with compassion, professionalism, and confidentiality.
