Section 2: Project Overview
2.1 Background
GlobeWest is a premium Australian furniture and homewares brand operating primarily in the
trade and B2B market. The current AU store operates at globewest.com.au on an Adobe
Commerce (Magento 2) instance. The business is expanding to the United States, with physical
showrooms planned for Texas, California, and Florida from September 2026, and a dedicated
US ecommerce store to follow.
This brief covers Phase 1 of the engagement: front-end accessibility remediation of the existing
AU store to achieve WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliance. OD is concurrently delivering a brand
refresh to the AU site — this is OD-owned work and is not part of the delivery partner's scope.
The accessibility audit will be conducted collaboratively between OD and GlobeWest against the
refreshed AU site, and the resulting audit report is the complete input and specification for the
delivery partner's Phase 1 work. No independent audit or design work is required from the
